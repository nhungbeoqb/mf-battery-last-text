The phone battery's last text message to the charger it waited for all day — one line, 156 characters. A loyal battery that kept every alarm, photo and open tab, only to watch the plug go to the AirPods first. It forgives the charger. The AirPods, never.

> I waited all day, kept your alarms, your photos, your 47 open tabs, and you gave the plug to the AirPods first. I forgive you. Them, never. Goodbye from 1%.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
